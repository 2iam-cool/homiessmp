Homies | SMP is a Minecraft survival multiplayer server whose history is marked by ambitious construction projects, expanding settlements, farms, faction-building, rivalries, and several memorable disasters. This page documents the server's history in three broad eras, alongside dedicated pages for its factions, builds, and locations.

This wiki follows the event order supplied by the server's chronicle. Exact dates are shown only when known; undated events are not assigned invented dates.

Early history — Early Days

The server was created on August 11. Early activity centered on spawn, where the Bakery, the first enchantment area, “Pussy Cove”, and the “Cave” were established. The early period also saw the construction and destruction of the Bakery, TK's giant flaming T, and the beginning of Kaden's feud with the mountain surrounding spawn.

See the full Early Days article for the complete list of recorded events.

Pre-End Dimension Era

As the server expanded, settlements such as New Gurp City, Plaintiff, Sakura Sanctuary, and TK Land emerged. Farms, roads, railways, and public infrastructure became increasingly important.

See the full Pre-End Dimension Era article.

Age of Elytra / Post-Enderdragon Fight

The End dimension opened and the Ender Dragon was defeated, with three casualties recorded. Olin secured the server's first four elytras and the dragon egg. The following period brought new factions, large-scale construction, Nether travel infrastructure, and the incident at Abdiel's base that resulted in Kaden's in-world conviction and repayment obligation.

See the full Age of Elytra article.

The Bakery

Spawn landmark and Kaden's base

The Bakery was an early build at spawn and served as Kaden's base. Its history is notable for repeated destruction and reconstruction.

History

  • The Bakery / Kaden's base was completed during the earliest server period.
  • It was blown up at spawn by a trapped chest.
  • Kaden rebuilt the Bakery.
  • It later burned down due to lightning.

Spawn landmarks

Central area · early server history

Spawn was the site of several of the server's earliest public builds and later underwent a major overhaul.

Recorded landmarks

  • First enchantment area at spawn.
  • “Pussy Cove” and the “Cave”.
  • TK's giant flaming T.
  • Bakery / Kaden's base.
  • Statue of Dih overlooking spawn.
  • “Brick building” construction began at spawn.

Spawn overhaul

During the Pre-End Dimension Era, an overhaul of spawn began. The surrounding mountain was excavated and buildings were removed. Kaden's long-running feud with the mountain had begun in the earliest period.
